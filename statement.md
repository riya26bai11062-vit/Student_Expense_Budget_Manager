# Project Statement

## Project Title

Student Expense & Budget Manager

## Problem Statement

College students often have to manage their daily expenses while staying within a fixed monthly budget. Keeping track of small expenses manually can make it difficult to understand where money is being spent and whether the monthly budget is being followed.

The Student Expense & Budget Manager is developed to provide a simple Python-based solution for recording and managing student expenses, setting a monthly budget, and analyzing spending patterns.

## Proposed Solution

The system provides three main functional modules:

1. Expense Management
2. Budget Management
3. Analytics & Reports

The Expense Management module allows users to add, view, search, update, and delete expense records.

The Budget Management module allows users to set a monthly budget and check the total amount spent and remaining budget.

The Analytics & Reports module provides information such as total spending, category-wise spending, highest spending category, average daily spending, and a monthly summary.

The system also uses JSON file storage so that expense and budget data can remain available after the program is closed.

## Target Users

The primary users of this system are college students who want to manage and monitor their daily expenses and monthly budgets.

## Expected Outcome

The project aims to provide a simple, user-friendly, and modular system that helps students maintain expense records, monitor their budget, and understand their spending patterns.
